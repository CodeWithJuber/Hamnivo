---
title: Get started in 10 minutes
description: Sign up, let the guided setup walk you through, and put the chat on your website
---

You set everything up in **Hamnivo Studio**, the web page where your team works. You don't need
to be a developer.

## Step 1: Create your account

1. Open Studio (your Hamnivo address, such as `app.example.com`) and click **Create an account**.
2. Enter your email and a password.
3. Open the email Hamnivo sends you and click the link in it.

## Step 2: Create your workspace

Your workspace is your business in Hamnivo. Enter its name and press create.

**What you'll see:** the guided setup, **Welcome to Hamnivo**, opens by itself.

## Step 3: Follow the guided setup

The setup has seven steps. Every step can be skipped and done later, and nothing is lost if you
leave. To open it again later, go to **Home** and press **Open guided setup**.

1. **Workspace:** check your workspace's name.
2. **Brand and website:** name your chat and enter your website's address.
3. **Chat engine:** Hamnivo connects the chat engine for you.
4. **AI key:** paste a key from an AI company (OpenAI, Anthropic or Google) and press **Test** to
   check it works. Skip this if only people will answer.
5. **Test chat:** send a message to your own chat before any customer sees it.
6. **Install:** choose where your website is built (WordPress, WHMCS, Shopify, Google Tag Manager
   or plain HTML) and copy the code it shows.
7. **Team:** invite the people who will answer chats.

Each step, and what to click, is explained in [Getting started](/docs/setup/getting-started/).

## Step 4: Protect your account

1. Open the account menu at the bottom of the menu and choose **Account security**.
2. Add an authenticator app or a passkey.

## Step 5: Teach the AI about your business (optional)

1. Go to **AI assistant** → **Knowledge**.
2. Upload your FAQs, policies or product information, or add your website's address so the AI
   reads it. See [Teach your AI your website](/docs/ai/teach-your-ai-your-website/).

## Step 6: Hear new chats

1. Open **Inbox** and select **Alerts** (the bell).
2. Pick a sound and allow desktop notifications.

See [Sounds and notifications](/docs/inbox/sounds-and-notifications/).
