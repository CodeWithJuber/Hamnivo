---
title: Get started in 10 minutes
description: Sign up, create your workspace, and put the chat on your website
---

Get Hamnivo running in a few minutes. You'll have a working chat on your website.

## Step 1: Sign up

Go to `app.yoursite.com` and click **Create an account**.

1. Enter your email and a password
2. Click the link in the email Hamnivo sends you
3. You're back in Studio

**What you'll see:** The home page with a checklist of setup steps.

## Step 2: Create your workspace

Your workspace is your company in Hamnivo.

1. On the welcome screen, enter your company name
2. Click **Create workspace**

**What you'll see:** You're logged into Studio. The sidebar shows Home, Inbox, and Settings.

## Step 3: Turn on two-step verification

This is quick and keeps your account safe.

1. Click the account menu (bottom left) → **Account security**
2. Click **Add a security key** or **Add an authenticator app**
3. Follow the steps
4. Save your recovery codes somewhere safe

**What you'll see:** A checkmark next to "Two-step verification" on the account menu.

## Step 4: Add your brand and website

A brand is the chat for one of your websites.

1. Go to **Settings** → **Brands and install**
2. Click **Create brand**
3. Enter a name (e.g., "My shop")
4. Enter the website address (e.g., `https://www.myshop.com`)
5. Click **Create**

**What you'll see:** Your brand card, with an install code ready to copy.

## Step 5: Connect Chatwoot

Chatwoot stores the conversations. Studio sets this up for you.

1. On your brand card, click **Connect Chatwoot**
2. Studio shows what it will set up
3. Click **Connect** to confirm

**What you'll see:** "Connecting..." turns to "Connected". A green checkmark appears.

## Step 6: Publish an AI agent (optional)

Skip this if you want only people to answer.

1. Go to **AI assistant** → **AI agents**
2. Click **Create agent**
3. Give it a name (e.g., "Customer helper")
4. Click **Publish**

**What you'll see:** The agent is "Running". It's ready to help with questions.

## Step 7: Add your knowledge (optional)

If you published an AI agent, give it something to know.

1. Go to **AI assistant** → **Knowledge**
2. Click **Upload document**
3. Upload a PDF, Word file, or text file with your FAQs, policies, or product info
4. The agent now has this knowledge when customers ask

**What you'll see:** Your document in the knowledge list, marked "Ready".

## Step 8: Install the chat on your website

Copy the code from your brand and paste it into your website.

1. Go to **Settings** → **Brands and install**
2. Click on your brand
3. Copy the **install code** (it looks like `<script...`)
4. Paste it into every page of your website, just before `</body>`

**What you'll see:** A chat button appears in the bottom right corner of your website.

## Test it

1. Open your website in a new tab
2. Click the chat button
3. Send a test message
4. In Studio, go to **Inbox** and you'll see your message there
5. Reply to it in Studio

**What you'll see:** Your reply appears in the chat on your website.

## Invite your team

1. Go to **Settings** → **Team and access**
2. Click **Invite**
3. Enter their email and choose their role
4. They get an email and can join

**What you'll see:** Their name in the team list. Now they can answer chats in Inbox.

## Next steps

- **Customize the chat color**: Brand settings on your brand card
- **Set up AI better**: Add more knowledge, connect a real AI model (see [AI setup](/docs/ai))
- **Create custom roles**: Fine-tune who can see what
- **See reports**: Go to **Reports** to see which chats AI handled, which people handled

You're done. Your team can now answer customers from one place.
