---
title: Glossary
description: Key terms in Hamnivo
---

## AI agent

A software helper that reads customer messages and can suggest or give answers. In Hamnivo, an AI agent:

- Reads your knowledge (files you upload)
- Answers questions from that knowledge
- Hands off to a person when needed
- Can be turned off quickly with the Stop AI button

Example: An AI agent for a shop answers "How much does shipping cost?" from your uploaded shipping policy.

## Brand

A brand is one website's chat. Your website has a brand. If you run five websites, you have five brands, and each one has its own chat, AI agent, and conversations.

Each brand has:
- A name (e.g., "My shop")
- A list of allowed websites
- Its own AI agent (optional)
- A color and logo for the chat

## Chat widget (or just "chat")

The chat window that sits on your website. Your customers click it to send messages. It's what they see — nothing more.

The widget:
- Loads with one line of HTML code
- Works on phones and computers
- Sends messages to Hamnivo Studio
- Shows replies from your team or AI

## Handoff

When an AI agent stops answering and a person takes over. Example: A customer asks a question the AI can't answer. The AI says "Let me connect you to someone" and hands off to your team.

## Hamnivo Studio (or just "Studio")

The dashboard where you and your team work. It's where you:
- Read and reply to customer messages in the Inbox
- Manage AI agents and knowledge
- Invite your team
- See reports and settings

Only your team can log into Studio. Your customers never see it.

## Knowledge

The documents you upload to teach your AI agent. Knowledge can be:
- PDFs or Word files (policies, guides, FAQs)
- Text files or HTML
- Anything your AI agent should know to answer questions

When a customer asks a question, the AI searches your knowledge to find the answer.

## Memory

What Hamnivo remembers about a customer across conversations. Example: A customer once said "I'm allergic to peanuts." Hamnivo remembers this, so next time they chat, the AI or your team knows already.

## Workspace

Your company in Hamnivo. It's where your brands live, your team works, and your customers chat.

One workspace = one company = all your websites' chats.

## Chatwoot

The engine that stores conversations. It runs behind Hamnivo Studio. You don't need to open it or know about it — Studio handles everything.

Think of it as the database. Hamnivo is the dashboard on top.

## Role (or "team role")

What someone can do in Hamnivo. Example roles:
- **Admin**: Can do everything
- **Support agent**: Can answer chats, see knowledge
- **Knowledge editor**: Can upload and manage files for AI
- **Analyst**: Can see reports only

You choose who gets which role when you invite them.

## Inbox

The list of all chats waiting for your team or currently assigned to you. You see:
- How many customers are waiting
- Which chats are assigned to you
- Which ones AI has answered
- Which ones need a person

## Stop AI

A red button in Studio's header. Click it to pause the AI immediately. Customers won't get AI replies until you turn it back on.

Use it when:
- The AI is giving wrong answers
- You're updating your knowledge
- You want only people to answer for a while

## Custom field

Information about a customer you want to save. Example custom fields:
- "Customer type" (new, returning, VIP)
- "Account number"
- "Product they own"

Your team can fill in custom fields when talking to customers, and Hamnivo remembers them.
