Hamnivo is a customer chat platform where your team answers customers, AI assists, and everything is in one place.

## What is Hamnivo?

Hamnivo brings together conversations, people and AI agents. Your customers chat on your website. Your team answers in **Hamnivo Studio**. AI can help with answers, and when a customer needs a person, handoff happens instantly.

No juggling between apps. No transcribing. Everything is verified, nothing is guessed.

## Which app do I use?

You and your team work in **Hamnivo Studio**. That's the only app you need day to day.

### For your team

- **Hamnivo Studio** (`app.yoursite.com`): Where you and your team answer customers, manage AI, set up everything
- You log in with email + two-step verification
- Your role determines what you can see and do (admin, support agent, knowledge editor, etc.)

### Under the hood

- **Chatwoot** (at `support.yoursite.com`): Stores the conversations. You don't need to open it.
- **Platform console** (`app.yoursite.com/platform`): Only for the person who runs your server.
- **Chat widget**: Lives on your website. Your customers use it. They never see Studio or Chatwoot.

## What you can do with Hamnivo

- **Answer customers in Studio's Inbox**: Read messages, type replies, see what happened before
- **Connect an AI agent**: Upload your knowledge, let AI answer common questions
- **Add your team**: Invite people, give them roles and permissions
- **See what happened**: Reports show you which conversations were handled by AI, which by people
- **Install on your website**: One line of HTML, no coding needed
- **Connect to Chatwoot**: Studio sets it up for you, one click
- **Export and import conversations**: Move from another system, keep your history
