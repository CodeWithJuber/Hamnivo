# Tickets

A ticket is a conversation that needs follow-up. It has a reference such as **#1042**, a state and
a priority. Its whole history stays in the conversation, so nothing lives in two places.

## Where tickets come from

- The **contact form**: a customer leaves a message while nobody can answer
  ([set it up](../setup/chat-widget-forms.md)).
- **Your team**: open any conversation, choose **More actions**, then **Make this a ticket**. You
  can give it a priority straight away.

## Find tickets

1. Open **Inbox**.
2. Choose **Tickets** among the queues above the list.

Filter by state (**Open**, **Pending**, **Closed** or **All**) and by **Priority**. The filters stay
in the address bar, so you can bookmark or share a view. Each row shows the reference, the state
and the priority, always with words and not only colour.

## States

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Open** | Your team is working on it. |
| **Pending** | Waiting for the customer. It opens again when they write. |
| **Closed** | Done. It opens again if the customer writes. |

Above the conversation, choose the state next to the ticket's reference. Pending is the
conversation snoozed until the customer writes; Closed is the conversation resolved.

## Priority

Choose **Urgent**, **High**, **Medium**, **Low** or **No priority**. The priority is the same one
Chatwoot shows, so a change made there appears here too.

Good to know:

- Changing the state or priority needs a role that may change conversation status. Without it, the
  controls say why they are disabled.
- If someone else took the conversation while you were looking, the change is refused and nothing
  happens. Look again and try once more.
- A conversation never gets a second ticket number.
- The customer sees their ticket's reference and state in the chat.

## Ticket reports

**Reports → Tickets** shows, for the dates you choose:

- tickets created and closed, and how many are open right now;
- the median time from opening a ticket to closing it;
- tickets by priority and by source (contact form, staff, automation).

**Reports → Quality** also counts missed chats (the customer wrote and nobody answered within an
hour), contact form messages and how often closed chats are opened again. Small groups are hidden,
as everywhere in Reports, so no single customer can be picked out.
