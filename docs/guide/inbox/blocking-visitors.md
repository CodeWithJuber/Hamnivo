# Blocking visitors

Block a visitor who misuses your chat, for example with abuse or spam. A blocked visitor sees
only "Chat isn't available right now." They are never told they were blocked.

## Block from a chat

1. Open the conversation in the **Inbox**.
2. Open **More actions**, then **Block visitor…**.
3. Write the **Reason**. Your team sees it; the visitor never does.
4. Choose **How long**: 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days, or until someone unblocks them.
5. Leave **Also close this chat** ticked to close the conversation at the same time.
6. Choose **Block**.

## Block from Live visitors

On **Live visitors**, choose **Block** on the visitor's row. If they open the chat from that
browser, they cannot write.

## What a block covers

- The customer behind the chat and the browser they used.
- For a customer your website signed in: their account, on every device.
- Hamnivo never stores IP addresses. A visitor who is not signed in and clears their browser can
  come back. Block them again if they do.

A blocked visitor can still:

- search your help articles;
- read their earlier conversations;
- change their privacy and email choices.

They cannot start a chat, send a message, use the contact form or reopen a chat.

## Unblock

1. In the **Inbox**, choose **Blocked** next to **Live visitors**.
2. Find the visitor. **Active** shows current blocks; **All** shows the last 90 days.
3. Choose **Unblock** and confirm. They can chat again at once.

A block also ends by itself when its time is up.

## Who can block

Administrators and supervisors. In custom roles, this is **Block and unblock visitors who misuse
the chat**. Every block and unblock is in the audit log.
