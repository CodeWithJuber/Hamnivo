# Sounds and notifications

Studio tells you when a customer needs you, even when you are working in another tab or window.
This page shows what you get and how to set it up. The settings are yours alone: each person on
your team keeps their own.

## What you get

| When this happens | You get |
|---|---|
| A chat is waiting for a person | A sound, a desktop alert, and the chat counts on your browser tab |
| A customer writes in your chat, or in a chat nobody has taken yet | A sound, a desktop alert, and a number next to the chat |
| Someone assigns or transfers a chat to you | A sound and a desktop alert |
| A chat is [offered to you](offers.md) to accept or decline | The "assigned" sound and a desktop alert |
| A chat goes past its [reply-time target](reply-targets.md) | The "waiting" sound and a desktop alert |

You only hear about chats you can answer. A chat the AI is handling does not make a sound until a
person is needed. In a team that offers chats to one person at a time, the rest of the team hears
about a chat only when nobody accepted it.

You do not get a sound for a message in the chat you are reading right now, with Studio in front.

## Change your sounds

1. Open **Inbox**.
2. Select **Alerts** (the bell) above the list of chats.
3. Under **Sound for each event**, pick a sound for each of the three events. Choose **No sound**
   to keep one of them quiet.
4. Select the play button next to a choice to hear it.

There are five sounds: Chime, Pop, Bell, Drop and Ping.

## Volume and mute

1. Open **Inbox**, then **Alerts**.
2. Move the **Volume** slider. The change is saved when you let go.
3. To silence every sound, turn on **Mute all sounds**. Desktop alerts and the count on the tab
   keep working.

## Desktop alerts

A desktop alert is a small box that appears on your screen when Studio is in the background.
Select it to open the chat.

1. Open **Inbox**. If Studio has not asked yet, a box says **Hear about new chats while Studio is
   in the background**. Select **Allow desktop alerts**.
2. Your browser asks for permission. Choose **Allow**.
3. To check that it works, open **Alerts** and select **Send a test alert**.

You can turn desktop alerts off in **Alerts**, and you can hide the start of the customer's message
from them (useful if other people can see your screen).

**If your browser says the alerts are blocked:** open your browser's settings, find the
notification settings for this site, allow notifications, and reload Studio. Studio cannot change
this for you.

## Unread chats

- A chat with new customer messages shows the customer's name in bold and a number for how many
  messages you have not read.
- The number goes away when you open the chat and look at it. You do not have to do anything else.
- Reading is yours alone: a teammate still sees the chat as unread until they read it.
- The browser tab shows how many chats need you, like **(3) Inbox · Hamnivo Studio**, and the tab's
  icon has a red dot with the number.

## Your status

Above the list of chats, **Your status** says whether you take chats now:

- **Online**: you are using Studio.
- **Away**: you chose it, or Studio has been open but not used for about five minutes.
- **Offline**: you chose it, or Studio is closed.

Customers who ask to talk to a person are told "a person from our team is online" only when
someone who can answer that brand is online. Otherwise they are told the team will reply. When you
choose who to assign a chat to, each name shows whether that person is online, away or offline.

## Typing

You see **Maya is typing…** under the chat while the customer writes. The customer sees that you
are typing while you write a reply. They never see it while you write a private note.

## If you hear nothing

1. Check that **Mute all sounds** is off and the volume is not 0.
2. Check that the event you expect has a sound, not **No sound**.
3. Browsers keep a page silent until you have clicked on it. After opening Studio, click anywhere
   on the page once.
4. Check that your computer's sound is on and the browser tab is not muted.
5. Open **Alerts** and play a sound to test it.
