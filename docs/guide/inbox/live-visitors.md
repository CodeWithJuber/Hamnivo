# Live visitors

**Live visitors** shows who is on your website right now, so you can see where people are and say
hello first. It lives next to the inbox.

## Open it

1. Open **Inbox**.
2. Select **Live visitors** above the list of chats.

Select **Back to inbox** to return.

## What the list shows

The list updates by itself every few seconds. It shows one line for each person who has a page
of your website open:

| Column | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Visitor** | The customer's name if they have given one in the chat, otherwise "Visitor" and a short code. The brand's name is under it. |
| **Page** | The title of the page they are on and its address. Select it to open it in a new tab. |
| **Time on site** | How long this visit has lasted. |
| **Country** | Where in the world they are, when it is known. |
| **Device** | Phone, computer or tablet, with the browser and system. |
| **Visits** | **New**, or **Returning** with the number of visits so far. |
| **Actions** | **Open chat** when the visitor is in a chat with you, or **Start chat**. |

Above the list, **On the website now** gives the count. If you look after more than one brand, use
**Brand** to show one of them.

You only see visitors of the brands your role covers. A visitor whose browser sends **Do Not Track**
or **Global Privacy Control** never appears, and a visitor who closes the page leaves the list
within a few minutes.

## Start a chat

You can invite a visitor to chat if your role lets you reply to customers.

1. Find the person and select **Start chat**.
2. Write what you want to say. Studio suggests "Hi! Can I help you find something?". You have
   300 characters.
3. Select **Send invitation**.

Within a few seconds your message appears next to the chat button on the page the visitor is
viewing, the same way your brand's own prompts do. If they select it, the chat opens with your
words as the greeting.

Good to know:

- An invitation waits for **3 minutes**. After that it is gone.
- Nothing appears in your inbox until the visitor writes something. Then it is an ordinary chat.
- If the visitor leaves before you send, Studio says they have left the website.
- Each invitation is recorded in the audit log, with who sent it and what it said.

## If the list is empty

You see "Nobody is on your website right now" when no visitor has a page open. If you expect
visitors and see none:

- The chat script must be installed on your website (see **Settings, Brands and install**).
- Page tracking must be on for the brand (see below).
- Search engines and scripts are left out on purpose.

## Page tracking for a brand

If you can configure the chat widget, **Visitor tracking** at the bottom of the page lets you turn
page tracking off for a brand, and choose whether the part of a page address after a question mark
is kept. See [Visitor details](visitor-details.md) for what is kept and what customers are told.
