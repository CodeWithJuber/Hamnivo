# Consent before chatting

Your chat can ask customers to agree before a conversation starts, for example to follow the GDPR.

## Open it

1. Open **Settings** in the menu, then **Chat widget**.
2. Choose your brand under **Brand**, then the **Privacy** tab.

## Who is asked

Under **Who is asked**, choose:

- **Nobody**: customers start chatting straight away.
- **Every visitor**: everyone agrees once, before their first message.
- **Only visitors from the EU, EEA and UK**: Hamnivo checks where the connection comes from on each
  visit and never stores it. When the country is not known, the visitor is asked, to be safe.

## The words customers agree to

Write the words in **The words customers agree to**. Left empty, Hamnivo's own words are used,
with your brand's name in them. English is required if you write your own; other languages show
the English until you write them.

The chat shows the words, your **Privacy policy link** (set on the **Look** tab) and an **I agree**
box that is never ticked in advance. A chat cannot start until the customer agrees.

## When you change the words

Each saved version of the words has a number. Customers who agreed to an earlier version are
asked again before their next new chat. Studio tells you this before you save.

Good to know:

- Hamnivo keeps which version a customer agreed to and when, never their country.
- Customers who do not agree can still search your help articles.
- Turning consent on affects new chats; conversations already open go on.
