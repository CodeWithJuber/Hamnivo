# Business hours

Business hours tell your customers, honestly, when a person on your team can answer. Outside
those hours the chat says nobody is online, and when your team is back. **Your AI assistant
answers at any hour.** The hours only decide what the chat says about people.

## Open the page

1. Open **Settings** in the menu, then **Business hours**.
2. If you have more than one brand, choose the **Brand**. Each brand has its own hours.

You can also reach it from a brand's card on **Brands and install**: press **Edit business hours**.
Only an owner or administrator can change the hours. Others can look.

## Set your hours

### Choose the time zone

Under **Time zone**, choose the one your team works in. All the times you set are in this time
zone, wherever your customers are. A new brand starts with the time zone of your computer.

### Quick start

Three buttons fill the week in one press:

- **Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00**
- **Every day, all day**
- **Closed every day**

After pressing one, change anything you like.

### Set each day

Each day has its own box.

1. Tick **Open** for each day your team answers. A day that is not ticked is closed.
2. Set **Opens** and **Closes**.
3. For a break in the middle of the day, press **Add another time**, and set the second time. For
   example, open 9:00 to 13:00 and again 14:00 to 18:00. Times on one day must not overlap.
4. To take away a time, press the cross next to it.

A closing time of **12:00 AM** means midnight, at the end of that day. So "open 9:00 AM, close
12:00 AM" is open until midnight.

### Days closed

Public holidays and other whole days when nobody answers.

1. Under **Days closed**, choose a **Day**.
2. Press **Add day**.
3. To take a day away, press the cross next to it.

On a closed day the chat says nobody is online, whatever the weekly hours say.

### Save

Press **Save hours**. Studio checks the times first and says what to fix, for example: "On Tuesday,
closing must be after opening." If you leave the page with changes that are not saved, Studio
asks first.

## What customers see

| When | What the chat does |
|---|---|
| Your team is working. | A customer who asks for a person is told the request is with the team. |
| Your team is away. | The customer is told the team is away and when it answers again, such as "Monday 9:00 AM", in the customer's language and your time zone. Their messages are kept, and they can keep chatting with the AI assistant. |
| It is a day closed. | The same as away. |

You can write your own words for the away message. See [Message when nobody is
online](widget-look.md#message-when-nobody-is-online).

## Remove the hours

Press **Remove hours** and confirm. With no hours set, the chat never says that a person is
available right now. It still accepts a request for a person, and tells the customer it is with
your team. You can set hours again at any time.

## Check it

Open the [test chat](test-chat.md) and ask for a person, with the preview set to a time when your
team is away. Or change the hours to make "now" a closed time, and check what the chat says.
