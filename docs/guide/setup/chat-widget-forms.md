# Questions before a chat, and a contact form

Your chat can ask a few questions before a conversation starts, and offer a contact form when
nobody can answer. Both are set per brand, in four languages.

## Open it

1. Open **Settings** in the menu, then **Chat widget**.
2. Choose your brand under **Brand**, then the **Forms** tab.

Only people whose role may configure the chat widget can change the forms. A picture of the chat
on the right shows each form as customers will see it: choose **Before a chat starts**, **Contact
form** or **After sending** under **Show**, and a **Language**.

## Before a chat starts

Turn on **Ask these questions before a chat starts** and choose the questions. Customers answer
them in a **Before we start** card, then the chat begins.

- **Kinds of answer**: Name, Email address, Phone number, Their question, Team to talk to, Short
  text, or Pick from a list. Each of the first five can be asked once.
- **Team to talk to** lists answers that each lead to one of your **Support teams**. The
  conversation goes straight to that team. Add a team first under **Settings**, **Support teams**.
- **Their question** becomes the customer's first message, so your team and the AI assistant see
  it at once.
- **Customers must answer this** makes a question required. The widget says "(required)" in words.
- **Skip the questions for customers your website signed in**: your website already told us who
  they are, so they go straight to the chat.
- A form can ask at most 6 questions. Every extra question means fewer customers start a chat.

What happens to the answers:

- Your team sees them in a private note at the start of the conversation, which the customer never
  sees.
- The name is shown in the inbox instead of "Visitor".
- An email address the customer typed is marked **not verified**. It is never treated as their
  confirmed address.

## When nobody can answer

With **Show a contact form when nobody can answer** on, the chat offers **Leave a message** when:

- **Outside business hours, or when nobody is online** (the default),
- **Only outside business hours** (needs [business hours](business-hours.md)), or
- **Only when nobody on the team is online**.

The contact form always asks for an email address and the customer's question: the answer reaches
them by email. You can add other questions, write your own **Thank-you message**, and choose **Let
the AI assistant answer first**.

What happens after a customer sends it:

1. The message becomes a [ticket](../inbox/tickets.md) in the inbox, with a reference such as
   **#1042**, waiting for a person.
2. The customer sees the reference and is told to check their email.
3. They get one email asking them to confirm their address. It never repeats what they wrote, so
   nobody can use your form to send someone else a message.
4. Once they confirm, your team's replies are emailed to them. They can also come back to the chat.

Good to know:

- Customers can still write in the chat while the form is shown; the AI assistant may answer.
- One address gets one confirmation email in 10 minutes, however many messages name it.
- To reply by email, customers open the chat again for now. Replying to the email itself comes with
  the email channel.

## Save

Press **Save forms**. Websites get the change within a minute. If you leave the page with changes
that are not saved, Studio asks first.
