# Test chat

The **test chat** is your real chat, running inside Studio. Use it to see what your customers will
see and what your AI assistant answers, before your website has the chat on it.

## Open it

1. Open **Settings** in the menu, then **Brands and install**.
2. Find your brand and press **Test chat**.

The same test chat is step 5 of the [guided setup](getting-started.md).

The button is greyed out, with a note, until the chat engine is connected for the brand: **Connect
the chat engine for this brand first.** Connect it on the same card, then come back.

## What to try

- Type a question a customer would ask. Your AI assistant answers.
- Ask for a person, for example "I want to talk to someone". The chat answers honestly, depending
  on your [business hours](business-hours.md): your request is with the team, or the team is away
  and when it is back.
- The test chat speaks the language Studio is in. Change Studio's language to see the chat in
  another one.
- After changing [how the chat looks](widget-look.md), save and open the test chat to see it.

If nothing answers, the AI agent may not be published yet. Open **AI assistant**, then **AI
agents**, and publish it with a real model. Customers get no AI answers until you do.

If the box stays empty or the chat will not load, ask whoever runs your Hamnivo server. The server
has to allow Studio to show the chat page.

## What a test chat is and is not

- **It is real.** The AI uses your real key and what your agent knows. A test chat is not a
  pretend one.
- **It stays out of your reports and counts.** Test chats are marked as tests, so the numbers on
  Reports and the counts in your inbox leave them out. They can still appear in the inbox list,
  where your team can answer them.
- **It uses your plan like any chat.** The messages count towards your plan's limits.
- **It is kept apart from customers.** The test chat has a session of its own, so it never mixes
  with a customer's chat on the same computer.

Only your own Studio can show the chat for a test. Other websites can show it only if they are on
the brand's allowed list.
