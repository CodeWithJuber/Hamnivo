# Direct chat link and QR code

A direct chat link opens your chat on a page of its own, without your website around it. Share it
in emails, on social media, or print its QR code on receipts and packaging.

## Turn it on

1. Open **Settings** in the menu, then **Chat widget**.
2. Choose your brand under **Brand**, then the **Share** tab.
3. Tick **Let people open the chat from a link** and press **Save link setting**.

The **Chat link** appears, with **Copy link** and **Open**, and the **QR code** with **Download QR
code**.

## What customers see

The page shows your brand's name and logo, a language choice and the chat. Everything works as on
your website: the questions before a chat, consent and the contact form.

Good to know:

- The link works even if the page is not one of your allowed websites.
- Search engines are told not to list the page, and no other website can show it inside a frame.
- **Turning the link off** ends the chats that were opened from it. Customers who open the link
  afterwards see that the chat could not start.
- The **Share** tab also has the install code for your website.
