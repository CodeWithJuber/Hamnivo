# Connect Telegram

Answer your Telegram bot's messages from the Hamnivo inbox.

## Make a bot

1. In Telegram, message **@BotFather** and send `/newbot`.
2. Choose a name and a username ending in `bot`.
3. Copy the **token** BotFather gives you. It looks like `1234567:AAH…`.

## Connect

1. Open **Settings → Channels** and choose **Connect Telegram**.
2. Paste the token. Hamnivo checks it with Telegram.
3. Choose whether the AI answers here (off at first) and which team gets these chats.
4. Choose **Connect**. The channel shows your bot's `@name`.

Customers start a chat by opening `t.me/<your bot's username>`.

## Good to know

- The token goes to your chat engine (Chatwoot), which keeps it. Hamnivo stores none.
- Telegram has no reply window: you can answer at any time.
- Product and order cards arrive on Telegram as text with a link.
